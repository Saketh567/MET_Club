/ ABOUT MET

Where theory meets
creation.

MET isn't just a club - it's a community of thinkers, builders, and dreamers. We bring together students from every discipline who share one belief: that the best ideas happen at the intersection of mathematics, engineering, and technology.

From algorithm workshops to robotics builds, from hackathons to research collaborations - we create spaces where curiosity is rewarded and ambition is accelerated.
