/ WHAT DRIVES US

Extremely Proud.

When we started the MET Club, it was just a small group of passionate students who wanted to bridge the gap between abstract mathematics and hands-on engineering. Today, we stand incredibly proud of the community we have built together.

We are proud of the late nights spent debugging code, the hardware that finally worked after the tenth iteration, and the friendships forged in the fires of our hackathons. Our strength lies in our diversity of thought, combining different disciplines to solve tomorrow's problems, today.
